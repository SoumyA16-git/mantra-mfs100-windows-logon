# Privacy Policy

**Mantra MFS100 Windows Hello Biometric System** is committed to protecting your privacy and security.

### 1. Data Collection
This software **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell** any personal data, user credentials, biometric templates, or usage telemetry.

### 2. Biometric Data Handling
- All biometric fingerprint scans are processed 100% locally on your computer.
- Minutiae templates are managed exclusively by the native Windows Biometric Framework (WBF) and stored within the operating system's native encrypted `WinBioDatabase` (`C:\Windows\System32\WinBioDatabase\`).
- No biometric data or plain-text user passwords are ever written to unencrypted disk storage or sent over the internet.

### 3. Network Communication
This software operates **100% offline**. It makes zero outbound internet connections and does not connect to any third-party cloud services or Aadhaar/UIDAI servers.
